Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to commonly asked questions about our bakery and ordering process

You can place an order through our website menu page by clicking the "Order" button on any product. Fill in the form and it will be sent directly to our WhatsApp. You can also directly message us on WhatsApp at +91 98765 43210 or call us to place your order.

Yes! We offer home delivery within a 15km radius of our bakery. Delivery is free for orders above ₹499. For orders below ₹499, a nominal delivery charge of ₹50 applies. We also deliver through Swiggy and Zomato.

For custom designed cakes, we recommend placing your order at least 48-72 hours in advance. For elaborate wedding or multi-tier cakes, please give us at least 1 week's notice. Regular cakes can be ordered with just 4-6 hours advance notice.

Absolutely! We offer eggless versions of most of our cakes and bakery items. Our eggless cakes are just as moist and delicious. Please mention your preference while ordering and we'll ensure your order is eggless.

We accept all major payment methods including Cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Wallets. For custom and large orders, we require a 50% advance payment at the time of order confirmation.

Yes, we love catering for events! Whether it's a corporate event, birthday party, wedding reception, or any celebration, we can handle bulk orders. Contact us with your requirements and we'll create a customized package with special bulk pricing.
